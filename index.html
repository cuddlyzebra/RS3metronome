Shifts exactly when a sync (tap or auto) lands. If a sync consistently fires too early -- e.g. auto-sync catching an ability's click animation rather than the cooldown actually starting -- raise this. If tap-to-sync consistently lands late because of your own reaction time, lower it (negative is earlier).
Auto-sync from XP drop (experimental)
Watches a small region of your screen for the sudden pixel change that happens the instant an XP drop appears, and re-syncs the metronome to that moment automatically. Calibrate this on a screen-fixed spot -- your XP orb near the top of screen, or the "+xp" popup in the RuneMetrics tab (with "Show precise values" and "Show XP change value" on) -- never the text that floats above your character, which moves with the camera and won't work here. Needs a one-time calibration since everyone's UI scale and layout differs.
Auto-sync from ability GCD (experimental)
Watches a small region of your screen for the sudden pixel change that happens the instant an ability's GCD cooldown starts sweeping across its icon, and re-syncs the metronome phase to that moment automatically. Needs a one-time calibration since everyone's ability bar sits somewhere different.
Transparent overlay counter
Draws the current count directly over the game (no window border, no background) at a spot you choose, so you can keep it in view without alt-tabbing to this app. Updates instantly, no fade or slide between numbers.
Click the center of your ability slot
This captures your current screen. Click directly on the ability icon whose cooldown sweep you want the metronome to watch (usually the first slot you press). Scroll to zoom in on the cursor for a more precise click. You can re-run this any time your UI moves or rescales.
